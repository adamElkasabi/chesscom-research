The Interview Journey

What to expect when applying at Chess.com

Recruiter screen

30-min intro video call with someone on our Talent team.

Hiring Manager interview

Deeper dive into the role and mutual fit with your future manager.

Take-Home assignment

Role-specific assignment (if applicable) completed on your own time.

Team interview

Meet the cross-functional teammates you'd work with day-to-day.

Final interview

A conversation with a senior leader to close out the process.
